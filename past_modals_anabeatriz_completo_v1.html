Exercício 1 — Practice

Como funciona

must have + particípio = tenho quase certeza que aconteceu  ·  can't / couldn't have = tenho certeza que NÃO aconteceu  ·  might / may / could have = talvez tenha acontecido

should have = deveria ter (arrependimento / crítica)  ·  shouldn't have = não deveria ter  ·  could have = poderia ter (e não fez)  ·  would have = teria (situação imaginária)

💡 Na fala: should've · could've · would've · must've (soa "shoulda", "coulda", "woulda")

Parte 1 — Complete as frases

Complete com um past modal + o verbo entre parênteses. Em algumas frases mais de uma resposta funciona.

  1. Jess texted me "running late!!" three times. She (get) stuck in traffic on the freeway.
  2. I (bring) a jacket! Nobody told me it gets this cold at night here.
  3. That (be) Mark at the studio this morning — he's in Mexico all week.
  4. I can't find the extra springs for the reformer. Someone (borrow) them, I guess.
  5. You (tell) me the store closed at 6! I drove all the way there for nothing.
  6. If I'd known how expensive healthcare was here, I (get) better insurance.
  7. No way she (finish) that whole cake by herself. It was huge!
  8. I (sign up) for that workshop, but I was too lazy to fill out the form.
  9. We (leave) the party so early — it only got fun after 11.
  10. Wow, your back looks so much better. Those exercises (help) a lot.
1. must have gotten  |  2. should have brought  |  3. can't have been / couldn't have been  |  4. might have borrowed / may have borrowed / must have borrowed  |  5. could have told / should have told  |  6. would have gotten  |  7. could have finished*  |  8. could have signed up  |  9. shouldn't have left  |  10. must have helped

*"No way" já é negativo, então o modal fica afirmativo: No way she could've finished it = "Ela não tem como ter terminado".
Obs.: no inglês americano, gotten é o particípio padrão de get.

Parte 2 — Escolha a opção correta

1. "I told her the class was at 9, not 10. She ______ confused."
2. "Why didn't you call me? I ______ you a ride to the airport!"
3. "Ugh, I ______ that second iced coffee. Now I can't sleep."
4. "Nobody answered the door. They ______ home."
5. "I'm not sure why Lucy left early. She ______ felt sick."
6. "If you had asked me, I ______ yes."
7. "Her flight landed at 3 and she's still not here. She ______ her connection."
8. Your client gives you a gift card after her last session. You say: "Oh my gosh, you ______!"
1. must have been  |  2. could have given  |  3. shouldn't have had  |  4. must not have been (também: can't have been)  |  5. might have  |  6. would have said  |  7. may have missed  |  8. shouldn't have

Nº 8: "You shouldn't have!" é uma expressão super comum pra agradecer um presente — tipo "Ai, não precisava!".

1. Your friend failed her driving test at the DMV. (should have)

2. A client cancelled 10 minutes before her session. (might have)

3. Your neighbor's car is full of boxes and there's a U-Haul in the driveway. (must have)

Respostas possíveis:

4. Seriously? I could've saved so much money!
5. No way. It can't have been her — she must've seen someone who looks like her.
6. I should've put on sunscreen. / I shouldn't have stayed in the sun all day.

Exercício 2 — Writing

Expressões-alvo

should've  ·  shouldn't have  ·  could've  ·  would've  ·  must've  ·  might've

Conectores úteis: looking back · honestly · to be fair · if I'd known · at the time · in the end · I guess

Parte 1 — Aquecimento: sobre você

Complete as frases falando de você. Não precisa ser formal — escreva do jeito que você contaria pra uma amiga.

Before moving to the US, I should have...

In my first months here, I shouldn't have worried so much about...

If I hadn't decided to take this year off, I would have...

If I hadn't studied physical therapy, I could have been...

The first time I taught Pilates in English, I must have...

Parte 2 — Leia o modelo

Leia este post curto. Repare em como os past modals aparecem de um jeito natural, no meio da conversa.

Things I'd tell myself one year ago

So, it's been almost a year since I hit pause on my career, and honestly? I should've done it sooner. I spent way too much time stressing about what people would think. Looking back, I shouldn't have cared so much — nobody was paying that much attention anyway.

There are things I could've done better, for sure. I could've saved more money before the break, and I probably should've made a plan for the first few months instead of just "going with the flow." I must've spent the whole of January binge-watching shows.

But if I'd stayed in my old job, I would've missed so much. I wouldn't have started reading again, and I might've never discovered how much I love cooking. So, no regrets. Well… maybe a few. 😅

Sublinhe mentalmente (ou anote abaixo) os past modals que você encontrou no texto. Quantos são?

9 past modals: should've done · shouldn't have cared · could've done · could've saved · should've made · must've spent · would've missed · wouldn't have started · might've never discovered
(+ "if I'd stayed" = if I had stayed, a 3ª condicional que costuma vir junto com would have.)

Parte 3 — Produção escrita: o seu "ano sabático"

Agora é a sua vez. Escreva um texto de 150 a 200 palavras, no estilo de um post ou de uma mensagem longa pra uma amiga, sobre este último ano: suas escolhas, o que você faria diferente e o que você ganhou com essa pausa.

Checklist: use pelo menos 5 past modals diferentes (should've, shouldn't have, could've, would've, must've, might've) e pelo menos 2 conectores da caixa lá de cima.

Resposta pessoal. Pontos pra checar na correção:
✔ verbo no particípio depois de have (should've gone, não "should've went")
✔ should've = arrependimento · could've = possibilidade que não foi aproveitada · would've = cenário imaginário
✔ if I had known…, I would have… (nunca "if I would have known")
✔ tom leve e natural, como conversa

Parte 4 — Mensagem rápida

Sua amiga te mandou esta mensagem. Responda com 3 a 4 frases, usando pelo menos 2 past modals (pra consolar, dar uma ideia ou especular o que aconteceu).

💬 "Girl, I'm so mad at myself. I missed my flight to Miami this morning. I set two alarms and still woke up at 9. The airline wants $300 to rebook. 😩"

Exemplo de resposta:
"Oh no, I'm so sorry!! Your phone must've been on silent or something. You could've called me — I would've driven you there, seriously! Don't be so hard on yourself, it could've happened to anyone. Maybe try calling the airline again, they might waive the fee."

Exercício 3 — Speaking

Expressões-alvo

Especular: she must've… · they can't have… · he might've… · maybe she…

Arrependimento / outros caminhos: I should've… · I shouldn't have… · I could've… · I would've…

Pra ganhar tempo falando: Hmm, let me think… · I mean… · I guess… · Honestly? · Now that I think about it…

Esta atividade é pra fazer em voz alta, na aula. Use os campos só pra anotar palavras-chave antes de falar — não precisa escrever frases completas.

Parte 3 — Storytime: a day that went wrong

Conte uma história real (ou inventada!) de um dia em que tudo deu errado — no trabalho, numa viagem, numa aula de Pilates, no mercado... A professora vai reagir e comentar usando past modals, e você responde também.

Exemplos de comentários: "Oh no, you should've…" · "You could've…" · "I would've…" · "That must've been so embarrassing!"

Roteiro rápido (palavras-chave):

How did it end?
